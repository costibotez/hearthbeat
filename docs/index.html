Hearthbeat

Peace of mind for the people you love.

Turn a Seeed Studio MR60BHA2 radar kit into a contact-free room monitor: heart rate, breathing, presence and sleep, with alerts on Telegram, in 9 languages. Installing takes about 5 minutes and needs only a phone.

Install with a phoneRecommended — for a new kit that still has its factory software

  1. Download the Hearthbeat file to your phone ⬇ Download hearthbeat.ota.bin

    Keep it in your Downloads / Files.

  2. Power the kit

    Plug it into any USB charger and look for a Wi-Fi network starting with seeedstudio-mr60bha2.

  3. Join that network and open the kit's page

    On the phone, connect to that Wi-Fi. A page opens by itself; if not, open http://192.168.4.1 in the browser. You do not need to enter your home Wi-Fi here.

  4. Upload the file

    Scroll to OTA Update, choose hearthbeat.ota.bin from Downloads and press Update. Wait about a minute until the page says it is done. Don't unplug the kit meanwhile.

  5. Connect Hearthbeat to your home Wi-Fi

    After the kit restarts, follow the Wi-Fi setup steps.

If .local addresses don't open on your phone (some Android phones), find the sensor's IP address in your router's list of devices and open that instead.

Install with a computerFor any kit, including ones already flashed with other software

  1. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer

    Firefox and Safari can't talk to USB devices.

  2. Connect the kit with a USB-C cable that carries data

    If it doesn't show up, hold the small BOOT button while plugging it in.

  3. Install This browser can't install over USB — use Chrome or Edge on a computer, or the phone method above. USB installs need a secure page (https).

    Pick the serial port, install, then enter your Wi-Fi when asked. Continue with Finish setting up the sensor.

Set up your Hearthbeat sensor

  1. Connect the sensor to your home Wi-Fi

    Power the sensor and join hearthbeat-xxxxxx in your phone’s Wi-Fi settings. Choose your home Wi-Fi (2.4 GHz) in the setup page and enter its password. If the page doesn’t open automatically, visit http://192.168.4.1. A preconfigured sensor can join home Wi-Fi directly.

  2. Name the room

    Reconnect your phone to home Wi-Fi and open http://hearthbeat-xxxxxx.local. Your browser asks you to sign in. Enter admin and hearthbeat on a fresh sensor. Tap Finish setting up this sensor to name the room and choose a password. The browser asks you to sign in again with the new password.

    Open sensor setup
  3. Check placement

    Put the sensor where you want it and stand or sit in the area it should monitor. In the setup checklist, wait for Presence detected and tap Placement checked.

  4. Send a test alert

    Connect Telegram in the checklist, send a test message, and tap I received it when it arrives. You can also finish setup without Telegram.

Adding another room? Open the room switcher on your first sensor and tap Add a sensor. Match the device ID, choose a room name and emoji, and confirm with Add a sensor.

After installing

  1. Updates come to you

    Each sensor checks for new versions. When one is out, Settings → Software update shows it — one tap installs it.

  2. Alerts on your phone

    Create a Telegram bot with @BotFather and set it up in Settings → Telegram notifications on one sensor. Choose Use this sensor for the household and link your other rooms. Send /status to see the linked rooms in one reply.

Not a medical device. Hearthbeat gives peace-of-mind estimates from a radar. It does not replace medical care, a fall alarm or an emergency plan.
